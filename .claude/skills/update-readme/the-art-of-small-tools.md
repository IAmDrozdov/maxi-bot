# The Art of Small Tools

A short note on why the best tools are the ones you barely notice.

# Do one thing

A good tool does one job and does it well. A hammer does not try to be a saw, and nobody misses the saw when the nail needs driving.

# Stay out of the way

A small tool asks for little: no setup, no manual, no mood. You pick it up, the work gets done, and you put it down again.

# Leave a trace

When the job is finished, a good tool leaves something you can keep: a clean cut, a tidy page, a note for next time.
